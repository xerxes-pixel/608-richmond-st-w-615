Pricing Strategy

Not the staging. Not the photography. The price is the single most important decision we’ll make together.

05   PRICING STRATEGY
We Are Not the Market. The Buyers Are.
Neither of us sets the price. The market does. Our job is to read it accurately, present the data, and recommend a number that attracts the right buyers — not one that just feels good.

What We Bring to the Table

A detailed Comparative Market Analysis — recent sales, active competition, days on market, and price-per-square-foot trends in your specific area. We look at your home objectively, the way a buyer will.

A Word on Upgrades

“Did you make these upgrades for yourself — or for the next buyer?” Upgrades made before listing are about speed of sale, not always dollar-for-dollar value. We’ll help you understand what moves the needle.

09
YOUR PART

The Seller’s Role

A few simple things that make a real difference in how quickly — and how well — your home sells.

09   THE SELLER’S ROLE
The Home Has to Show Well. Every Time.
Buyers don’t schedule their emotions. The home needs to be ready — not just on good days, but consistently.

Clean and decluttered at all times

Buyers are trying to picture their life in your home. Make it easy for them.

Vacant during showings

Sellers present make buyers rush and skip honest conversations with their agent.

Staged and maintained

Staging recommendations directly impact how quickly — and for how much — your home sells.

Strong odours addressed

Pet smells, smoke, cooking — buyers notice immediately. Fresh air and neutral scents go a long way.

Top tip: pop the oven on low and throw in a little flour — it creates a subtle, warm, freshly-baked smell that makes a home feel inviting the moment buyers walk in.

11   FAQS & OBJECTIONS, HANDLED WITH CONFIDENCE
Straight Answers, Part One
“Why is your commission higher than the agent down the street?”

This was never about paying less — it’s about netting more. An agent who leads with a discount is telling you the only value they can offer is a lower number.

“I think my home is worth more than that.”

You might be right — so let’s check. We are not the market. The buyers are, and they vote with their offers.

“Why isn’t my home selling?”

Let’s let the market tell us. Ten days, zero showings — it’s the price. Ten showings, zero offers — it’s still the price.

“Should I make upgrades before I list?”

Sometimes — but for the right reason. Pre-listing work is about speed of sale, not added dollar value.

11   FAQS & OBJECTIONS, HANDLED WITH CONFIDENCE
Straight Answers, Part Two
“Will you tell buyers why I’m selling?”

Never. Your timeline, motivation, and situation stay with us — no matter what’s happening behind the scenes.

“Aren’t all agents basically the same?”

It’s the most common belief in real estate — and exactly why sellers shop on price. We’re happy to be measured against any agent you’re considering.

“Another agent said they could list it higher.”

Anyone can promise a higher number to win the listing. The market doesn’t honour promises — it honours buyers.

“Is now even a good time to sell?”

A motivated seller in any market beats a hesitant one. The question isn’t when. It’s whether you’re ready.
