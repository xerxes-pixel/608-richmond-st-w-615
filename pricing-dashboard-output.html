Act 2 · Part A

How buyers actually find your home.

Buyers don't browse listings randomly. They set a price range on Realtor.ca, and the search engine returns the homes that fit. Where you place your list price determines which searches include your home and which exclude it entirely.

The mechanic: A buyer searching $200K to $400K sees every listing inside that range. The moment your list price ticks above $500K, you disappear from their search. The buyer never sees the listing, never books a showing, never makes an offer.

One more thing the numbers above don't include: for every additional day your home sits on the market, you continue paying mortgage interest, property tax, utilities, insurance, and routine maintenance. A scenario that takes 60 days instead of 20 means roughly two additional months of carrying costs on top of any reduction in final sale price. Your actual carrying cost depends on your specific mortgage and property, but it's never zero, and it's never small.
